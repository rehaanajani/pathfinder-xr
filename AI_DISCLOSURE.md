# AI disclosure

**Tools used:** Claude (Anthropic) through Claude Code, as a coding assistant. Codex (OpenAI) as an independent code and evidence reviewer. Gemini and Grok were used for literature and prior-work search.

**What AI helped with**
- Literature search: finding prior work (TLIO, RoNIN, EqNIO, AirIMU, AirIO, KISS-IMU, Samsung's DVI-SLAM) and the TLIO headset dataset.
- Writing and refactoring code: the fault injector, the Kalman filter, the IMU voting logic, the training script, the benchmark and the report.
- Drafting documentation, which we then edited.
- Independent reviews of the entry and of our claims (saved prompts and answers; several bugs and over-stated claims were fixed because of them).
- The submission video: its animations are generated by our own script (`tools/video/render.py`) from our real data, and the narration is a synthetic voice (macOS text to speech) reading a script we wrote.

**What we decided and checked ourselves**
- The problem framing, the choice of approach (per-sensor fault isolation + camera health + a fault-aware learned motion model) and the scenarios to test.
- Every number in the README comes from a script run whose raw output is in `results/` (for the scored entry: `tools/eval_organiser.py` on the organisers' degradation script). Nothing is estimated by a language model.
- Every source we cite was opened and read. Claims we could not verify are not in the README.

**What no AI did**
- No AI model runs inside the system. The tracker is a Kalman filter plus a small network that we trained ourselves on public data.
- No result was generated or estimated by a language model.
